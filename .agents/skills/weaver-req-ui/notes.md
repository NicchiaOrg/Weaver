# Notes

## notes-view

- **Entry:** `Notes` in the sidebar, after Assets, icon `NotebookPen`. Like the other views but Settings, it shows Get Started until the project has `.weaver/`.
- **Header:** the view header of `requirements/ui#General`, title `Notes`; on its right only the save status, in muted text: `Saving…` while a write runs, `Saved` after it, or `Could not save: <reason>.` in `red` with `CircleX` until the next write succeeds. Nothing shows before the first change of the window's session.
- **Sheet:** the rest of the view scrolls as one page; the text sits in one column at most 760 px wide, centred, with 48 px above, 24 px on the sides and 96 px below. The whole column takes clicks: a click below the last block puts the cursor at the end. The editor is a `textbox` with `aria-multiline` and the accessible name `Notes`.
- **Empty:** while the sheet has no text, its first line shows in muted text `Write notes for this project. Start a line with [ ] for a checklist.`; it is not part of the text and disappears with the first character.
- **What shows:** WYSIWYG, as in Notion: Markdown markers never show, and the elements of `requirements/rules#notes-view` show formatted. Headings: level 1 at 24 px, level 2 at 20 px, level 3 at 16 px, all semibold, with space above; paragraphs at the base size with a line height of 1.6. Inline code and code blocks in the monospace font on the `muted` background, code blocks with no syntax colours and scrolling sideways inside their own box. Quotes with a 3 px `border` left rule; dividers as a 1 px `border` line.
- **Markdown shortcuts:** typed at the start of an empty line or paragraph, followed by a space, they turn the line into an element and disappear: `#`, `##`, `###` a heading; `-`, `*` or `+` a bulleted item; `1.` a numbered item; `[]`, `[ ]` an unticked checklist item and `[x]` a ticked one (also after `-`); `>` a quote. ```` ``` ```` followed by Enter or a space starts a code block; `---` followed by Enter a divider. Inline, `**text**`, `*text*` or `_text_`, `~~text~~` and `` `text` `` turn bold, italic, struck through and code as soon as the closing marker is typed. `Cmd+B`, `Cmd+I`, `Cmd+Shift+S`, `Cmd+E` (`Ctrl` elsewhere) do the same on the selection. `Backspace` at the start of a heading, quote or item turns it back into a paragraph. There is no `/` menu, no toolbar, no block handle and no drag of blocks.
- **Lists:** Enter in an item starts a new item of the same kind below, unticked for a checklist; Enter in an empty item ends the list. Tab nests the item under the one above, Shift+Tab takes it out one level. Undo and redo: `Cmd+Z` and `Cmd+Shift+Z` (`Ctrl+Z`, `Ctrl+Y` or `Ctrl+Shift+Z` elsewhere).
- **Checklists:** each item starts with a 16 px checkbox in the look of the shadcn `Checkbox`, aligned with the first line of its text. A click on it ticks or unticks the item without moving the cursor or the scroll; `Cmd+Enter` (`Ctrl+Enter` elsewhere) does the same on the item holding the cursor. A ticked item keeps its place; its text is struck through and in `muted-foreground`. The checkbox has the role `checkbox`, `aria-checked`, and the item's text as accessible name.
- **Links:** in the primary blue, underlined. A click puts the cursor in the link; `Cmd`+click (`Ctrl`+click elsewhere) opens it as `requirements/ui#General` (Markdown) says, `http` and `https` only, with its error toast. A link's tooltip shows its address and `Cmd+click to open` (`Ctrl+click to open`). An `http` or `https` address typed and followed by a space, or pasted alone, becomes a link.
- **Pasting text:** formatted text keeps the elements of `requirements/rules#notes-view` and drops everything else; pasted Markdown text is read as Markdown.
- **Images:** at their natural size, never enlarged, never wider than the column, as their own block. A click selects one, shown with a 2 px `ring` outline; `Backspace` or `Delete` then removes it. An image that cannot be shown (`requirements/rules#notes-view`) is a placeholder as wide as the column, 48 px high, in `muted` with a dashed `border`: `ImageOff` and the image's name, or its address, in muted text.
- **Adding images:** `Cmd+V` (`Ctrl+V` elsewhere) with images on the clipboard, or files dropped from the Finder or Explorer onto the sheet; while files are dragged over it, a 2 px primary line shows where they will go. Refusals are error toasts, one per refused file: `"<name>" is larger than 10 MB: notes images can be at most 10 MB.` and `"<name>" is not a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP image.`; a failed copy: `Could not add "<name>": <reason>.` Files that are not images show nothing.
- **Changed on disk:** when Weaver keeps the sheet's version, the warning toast `The notes changed on disk; Weaver kept your version.` Otherwise the sheet changes silently, keeping the cursor where the text around it still exists.
- **Trash:** an image the trash refused on leaving shows the error toast `Could not move "<name>" to the trash: <reason>` (`Could not move <n> images to the trash: <reason>` for several).
