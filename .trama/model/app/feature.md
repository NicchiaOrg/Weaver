# App

The desktop app around the projects: one process for every open project, one window per project, and the system notifications that bring the developer back. A project window shows, read-only, the Board with what waits for a decision and the Tasks still to finish, the Diagram of the Model, the Requirements, the Specs, the History, the list of the agent's jobs with their conversations, General, and Weaver's settings; every change to the Model and to the work goes through the developer's decisions and the agent's jobs. The Diagram, the Requirements and the Specs can show an open Proposal drawn over the current Model, and can focus on what a Proposal or a Task concerns.

Weaver stays next to the developer's own editor: wherever a file and a line are known, it opens them in the editor chosen in the settings.

The settings belong to the developer, not to a project: they live in the system's configuration folder and apply to every project; a project's files never change them.
